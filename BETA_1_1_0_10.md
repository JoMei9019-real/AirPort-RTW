# 1.1.0 Beta 10 — controlled 802.11v BTM

Branch `1.0.0-beta`; numeric version `1.1.0`.
DriverBuild: `1.1.0-beta.10-controlled-btm`. Stable main is unchanged.

## Scope and advertisement

Adds active BSS Transition Management (BTM), a subset of 802.11v. The association
request includes Extended Capabilities bit 19 only when the AP advertises BTM,
roaming assistance/switching/automatic policy are enabled, the dedicated control
timer is available, and the supported WPA2-PSK/CCMP non-PMF path is selected.
The supported Neighbor Report portion of 802.11k remains advertised separately.
No 802.11r FT, SAE, PMF, WNM Sleep or general 802.11v completeness is claimed.

`-rtwnoautoroam` in OpenCore boot-args disables automatic selection and BTM-driven
switching and suppresses the BTM bit after reboot. Manual switching remains
available. There is no runtime auto toggle. RTL8821CE defaults to automatic policy;
other cards remain opt-in through the personality settings inherited from Beta 9.

## BTM transaction

1. Validate the action frame source, destination, current association and bounded
   request parser. Rate-limit requests. Copy the intent; never disconnect in the
   receive handler.
2. A dedicated one-shot timer on the controller/RX workloop evaluates the intent
   under the existing power-transition hardware fence. Ordinary periodic roaming
   yields while BTM owns the transaction; AWDL channel changes are fenced too.
3. Require a candidate supplied by the AP, not the current BSSID, with nonzero
   preference if supplied, matching advertised/observed primary channel, fresh
   same-SSID RF observation, supported security and an enabled regulatory channel.
   DFS/no-IR still requires matching fresh beacon proof.
4. Normal requests retain the band-priority/radio policy and 6 dB same-band gain.
   AP preference orders otherwise suitable candidates. For disassociation-imminent
   requests, weaker usable candidates may be considered, but security, freshness,
   regulatory checks and switch cooldown are never bypassed.
5. If no fresh eligible result exists and at least 4.5 seconds remain, try at most
   one proposed enabled channel. Its result must retain the scan generation and
   complete without abort. Neighbor hints alone never authorize connection.
6. Send acceptance with the request token and target BSSID. Await the TX ACK
   matching the response's sequence and AP identity, up to 250 ms. Revalidate the
   target after the ACK, then invoke the existing WPA2 switch and one-AP fallback.
   Submission and ACK are distinct from successful association or IP traffic.

The candidate validity interval is interpreted in beacon intervals (TU converted
to milliseconds). Disassociation-imminent timers further shorten the deadline.
Local lifetime is capped at 30 seconds. Less than one second of join headroom is
rejected; this is a conservative admission rule, not a guarantee of completion.
The BTM timer runs only while an intent exists (100 ms control/scan checks,
20 ms ACK checks), is canceled on PM/teardown, and never creates a worker thread.

Requests without a preferred candidate list, empty lists, insufficient time,
invalid/unsupported candidates, active scan conflicts or cooldown can be rejected.
BSS termination schedules and ESS-leaving/URL modes are not executed; URLs are
never fetched. Only one target channel is tried; no unrestricted discovery loop.
Requests arriving while the existing scanner owns the off-channel PHY may not be
processed. AP implementations are allowed to disconnect despite a rejection.

Manual commands discard pending BTM intent. Link/key reset, source AP change,
power transition, replaced/aborted scans, response failure, missing ACK or expiry
cannot trigger a later stale switch. The old connection stays until an acknowledged
acceptance passes the final preflight; already-submitted joins retain normal
fallback behavior. This remains a full WPA2 join, not seamless Fast Transition.

## Diagnostics

IORegistry exposes `ROAM_V_ADVERTISED`, `ROAM_V_MODE` (`controlled-btm` or
`reject-only`), request/reject/accept/scan/switch/cancel counters. Advertisement
means the IE was built, not that an AP has independently verified every feature.
`ROAM_DIAGNOSTICS`/the report include BTM phase (0 idle, 1 evaluation/scan,
2 awaiting ACK), stage, request token, last response token/status, deadline,
remaining time, scan generation and target. Counters ending in submitted count
submissions; verify final BSSID, `switch_result` and usable traffic.

## Install and test

One ZIP directly contains `AirPortRTW.kext/`, executable `rtw88-roam` and these
notes. Replace the kext in `EFI/OC/Kexts/`, reboot and check DriverBuild. From the
extracted directory:

```sh
ioreg -r -c AirPortRTW -l | grep -E 'DriverBuild|ROAM_AUTO_ENABLED|ROAM_V_ADVERTISED|ROAM_V_MODE'
sudo ./rtw88-roam --log-level verbose
sudo ./rtw88-roam --watch 180 > ~/Desktop/AirPortRTW-Beta10-BTM-Watch.txt
sudo ./rtw88-roam --report > ~/Desktop/AirPortRTW-Beta10-After.txt
```

Use two reachable APs in the same network. Movement or the AP's steering policy
may cause a BTM request; advertising BTM does not force the router to send one.
Avoid manual scan/switch commands during the observation. A periodic client roam
does not prove BTM: look for `btm-request-received`, `btm-accept-submitted`,
`btm-response-acked` and the subsequent target connection. Check websites/LAN
traffic after a change. Requests may be validly rejected by the policy above.

## Validation limits

Host tests execute production BTM admission, wire response, target/security
validation, ACK binding, one-channel scan, switch invocation, timer PM fencing,
late rearm cancellation, deadlines and manual/source/power cancellation with
mocked OS/RF interfaces. Native compilation and stack checks do not validate
router interoperability, RF ACK reporting or sleep stability. No real BTM request
has yet been tested with this build. The prior Neighbor Report rejection and
unexplained Beta 7 boot panic remain open; Beta 9 diagnostics are retained.
